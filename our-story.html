On Friday December 10, 2021, at roughly 11:15 PM, Verity & Brandon met. What started as a coincidental meeting turned into a journey we’ve been on ever since.

We met just outside of The Milecastle on Westgate Road. Brandon had been out with a couple of friends for drinks and Verity had been out with their mutual friend, Kate, to see The Darkness at the old O2 Academy. Brandon had seen that Kate was in town on social media so messaged her to meet up as they hadn't seen each other for a while. So they agreed to meet outside The Milecastle.

When Brandon arrived he was greeted by Kate, but also by Verity. And he was immediately taken aback by Verity's beauty and energy, describing her and the moment as 'magical'
